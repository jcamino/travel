{kicker} Music you cannot see stateside · Silver Week 2026
# Silver Week Japan-Only Book
{lede} Tokyo Sat 19 – Tue 22 Sept, Kansai Wed 23 – Fri 25 Sept 2026. Two New Yorkers, Shibuya then downtown Kyoto. The test for everything here: could a Brooklyn resident see essentially this within a normal year without flying? Every event was read on the organiser's or venue's own page on 5–6 Sept 2026; one thing is booked, 高中正義 in Hikone on Fri 25. Fixed points: Sat 19 lands at 15:00, so the evening is open from about 17:30; Sun 20 and Mon 21 are free; Tue 22 is Mt Takao in the morning and **Swallows vs Hanshin at Jingu at 18:00** ([NPB](https://npb.jp/games/2026/schedule_09_detail.html)); Wed 23 the Shinkansen reaches Kyoto at 16:44; Thu 24 is free; Fri 25 is Arashiyama until about 15:00, then **高中正義 SUPER TAKANAKA LIVE in Hikone, doors 17:30, start 18:30, booked**, with an early airport train on Sat 26. Order of the page: the five best things of the whole week, ranked; then the best three each night; then everything else by category, with the per-day tables in section 5.
{legend} {v:VERIFIED} = read on the organiser's or venue's own page. {s:SECONDARY} = search snippet, blog, aggregator or inference. {t1:Tier 1} form/setting exists only in Japan · {t2:Tier 2} artist essentially never leaves Japan · {t3:Tier 3} exportable · {smp:SAMPLER} tourist product.

{note} **Two facts that shape the week, both verified.** (1) 21, 22 and 23 Sept 2026 are public holidays (敬老の日, 休日, 秋分の日) per the [Cabinet Office CSV](https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/syukujitsu.csv) {v:VERIFIED}. (2) **中秋の名月 (harvest moon) is Friday 25 September 2026**, with the astronomical full moon two days later on the 27th, per the [National Astronomical Observatory](https://www.nao.ac.jp/astro/sky/2026/09-topics03.html) {v:VERIFIED}. That puts the Kyoto/Osaka/Nara moon-viewing rites on your last night, which is also the Takanaka night in Hikone; every one of them is confirmed for the 25th below, and none can be combined with the concert.


## 0 · Top five for the whole trip, in order
{legend} Ranked across the whole week, not one per day: first by how far each is from anything Brooklyn can offer, then by whether you can get in. Jazz and everything else compete on the same terms; two of the five are jazz rooms. Two of them are the same Wednesday, as a pair: Yamashita if the lottery lands, the candle noh at 御香宮 if it does not. Where something fits before or after on the same day the entry says so. Sixth to eighth, for the record: Uchihashi & Ligeti at UrBANGUILD (Thu 24), Keiji Haino at 月花舎 (Sun 20) and the Kabuki-za 幕見 gallery (any Tokyo day); 友川カズキ (Tue 22) would be in the five if the Swallows game were dropped. Every night's own ranking is in section 0b. The harvest-moon rites led this list until the Takanaka tickets were bought on 6 Sept; they run while you are in Hikone, and sit in section 0b as what Friday would have been.

{meta} {t2:Tier 2} · booked · {v:VERIFIED} [takanaka.com/live](https://takanaka.com/live/) (doors, start, price), [bunpla.jp access](https://www.bunpla.jp/access/)
**What it is.** Takanaka has made records since 1972 and has never toured the United States; the closest a New Yorker gets is the reissues. This tour is three September halls (Katsushika 18 Sept, Hikone 25 Sept, Sendai 27 Sept) and the Hikone date is the one that fits the trip. A civic hall in a castle town on Lake Biwa, an audience that grew up with these records, ¥11,000 all reserved.
**Why it is first.** The ranking test is distance from Brooklyn, then whether you can get in. Takanaka is Tier 2 rather than Tier 1, but you hold the tickets, which no other entry on this page can say, and Tatsuro Yamashita below is the same kind of artist with no ticket. The cost is the harvest-moon rites, which is why this sat in section 3 until 6 Sept as "a Tier-2 legend on the wrong night".
**The evening.** Leave Arashiyama by about 15:00 (JR Saga-Arashiyama → Kyoto, 16 min), take the 新快速 from Kyoto toward Maibara (Hikone in about 50 min, four an hour), and be at the hall for doors at 17:30. Back the same way; trains toward Kyoto run past 22:30 {s:SECONDARY} (not read on JR's own timetable; check the last 新快速 on the day). Every Kyoto rite (Shimogamo 17:30, Daikaku-ji 17:30, Yasaka 19:00) runs while you are in the hall, so nothing of the 観月 is recoverable except the moon over Lake Biwa from the train and the walk home under it. Sat 26 is the early airport train.

{www} What: 山下達郎 "PERFORMANCE 2026", the annual hall tour of the city-pop originator who has never performed outside Japan; full band. | When: Wed 23 Sept · doors 17:00, start 18:00; ID checks from 16:15. {meta} {t2:Tier 2} · never abroad · {v:VERIFIED} [tatsuro.co.jp/live](https://www.tatsuro.co.jp/live/), [eplus.jp/tatsuro2026](https://eplus.jp/tatsuro2026/)
**What it is.** Yamashita, 73, one of the originators of city pop, does one hall tour a year (11 cities, 20 shows in 2026) and has never performed outside Japan. Osaka's Festival Hall is a 2,700-seat concert hall in Nakanoshima; open 17:00, start 18:00. It fits the schedule: the Shinkansen reaches Kyoto at 16:44, Shin-Osaka at 17:00; Midōsuji line to Yodoyabashi and a five-minute walk puts you in the hall by 17:30 with your bags (extend the ticket one stop, or go Kyoto → Osaka by JR special rapid, 30 min, and walk 10 min from Kitashinchi).
**Why it is second, not first.** It is the most Japan-only concert on this page, but you do not have a ticket and probably cannot get one. Two people need two accounts and two applications, each in its own draw.
**Practical.** Apply before **13 Sept 18:00 JST** at the e+ link above (try the e+ English portal first if the Japanese account refuses a foreign number). If it does not land, Wednesday is the candle noh at 御香宮 in Fushimi, 18:30, the next entry; the Osaka improv night at environment 0g (Kobi / 松本一哉 / 江崎將史, 19:00, ¥2,500+1D, cash at the door), 15 minutes from Festival Hall, is the third choice. Yamashita also plays ROHM Theatre Kyoto on 9 Oct, after you have left.

**What it is.** Gokōnomiya is the Fushimi shrine of the famous spring, and its noh stage is used for one candlelit performance a year. 「花月」 is the play of the boy Kagetsu at Kiyomizu-dera, with its small songs and kakko-drum dance: a 狂言 first, then a full noh with flute, two hand drums and a chorus, outdoors on a holiday evening, in a shrine that stages it as an offering. It is the only noh in Kansai that fits your days there; the Kyoto noh theatres have nothing on the 24th or 25th.
**Why it is fourth.** It clears the same bar as the harvest-moon rites, a performance inside a shrine by candlelight that no house in New York stages, and unlike Yamashita you can get in: B seats were still on sale on the shrine's page on 7 Sept and door tickets exist.
