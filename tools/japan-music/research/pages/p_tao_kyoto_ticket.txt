Theater Guidelines
Arrival
Arrive early to allow time for the elevators which can get busy before showtime. Latecomers will be seated at staff discretion during an appropriate break in the performance. No refunds for late entry.
Tickets
A 2D code ticket is required for entry. Please safeguard your ticket. Replacements cannot be issued for lost, damaged or duplicated tickets. All sales are final and tickets cannot be changed, cancelled, or refunded after purchase. Please check your order carefully before completing your booking.
ID Verification
Photo ID (passport, driver's license, or other government-issued ID) may be required at entry.
Performance Language
The performance is non-verbal. When required, English is used.
Photography & Recording
Photography, audio, and video recording are strictly prohibited during the performance.
Outside Food & Drinks
Outside food and drinks are not permitted in the theater.
Age Restrictions
Guests must be aged 6 or over. Guests must be 20 years or older to purchase alcohol.
Resale Prohibited
Ticket resale or transfer for profit is strictly prohibited. Entry will be refused for tickets purchased through unauthorized resale.
Performance Cancellations
Performances may be cancelled due to natural disasters, transportation disruptions, performer illness, or other circumstances beyond our control. In the event of cancellation, ticket prices will be refunded according to our standard process. Travel and accommodation costs are not covered.
Safety
Please remain seated during the performance. Running or disruptive behavior is not permitted. Guests who do not follow staff instructions may be asked to leave.
Important Booking Information
Seating & Age Restrictions
All seats are reserved. Guests must be 6 years or older.
Purchase Limits
Maximum 10 tickets per booking. Multiple bookings for the same performance are not permitted.
All Sales Final
Tickets cannot be changed, cancelled, or refunded after purchase. Please check your order carefully before completing.
Ticket Delivery
Tickets are sent digitally via the e-ticketing service lessQ. For more information
see:https://www.s2.e-get.jp/howto/resq/resq_en.html
Resale Prohibited
Ticket resale or transfer for profit is strictly prohibited. Entry will be refused for tickets purchased through unauthorized resale.
Same-Day Tickets
Available at the box office if seats remain.
Official Resale
There is no official resale service.
FAQs
How do I buy tickets?
Tickets are available to purchase online through our booking site.

When will tickets be available?
Tickets go on sale two months before the performance. Check our booking site to see current availability.
→Check availability
Can I choose my seat?
Yes. You can select and reserve your seat when purchasing tickets online.
→Reserve your seat
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept the following credit cards: VISA, Mastercard, JCB, American Express, Diners Club, UnionPay. We do not accept cash.
Are same-day tickets available?
Yes. Tickets can be purchased online until 10 minutes before show start.
→Buy tickets
Same-day tickets are also available at the theater box office (if seats remain) from 10 minutes before showtime.

Group bookings are available for 11 or more guests. Please contact us for details.

Can I cancel or change my ticket?
No. Tickets cannot be changed, cancelled, or refunded after purchase.
Will I get a refund if the performance is cancelled?
If the performance is cancelled due to natural disasters, performer illness, or other unforeseen circumstances, ticket prices will be refunded according to our standard process. Travel and accommodation costs are not covered.
