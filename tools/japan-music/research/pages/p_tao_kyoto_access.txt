FAQs
Is there parking?
The theater does not have dedicated parking. Please use nearby facilities or public transport.
Can I get there without getting wet in the rain?
Yes. The theater is directly connected to Kyoto Station via underground passage.
Will I make the last Shinkansen or airport bus after the show?
Yes. The theater is about 1 minute from Kyoto Station's Hachijo Exit via underground passage, with the limousine bus to Kansai Airport stopping right outside. If you are planning to catch the last Shinkansen toward Tokyo or the last limousine bus to Kansai Airport (both departing around 9:00pm), we recommend the First Show "HIBIKI" (7:00pm, ending around 8:00pm). After the Second Show "YUME" (9:00pm, ending around 10:00pm), trains toward Osaka are still running until after 11:00pm (about 30-50 minutes by JR), making it possible to continue on to Osaka or Kobe after the show.
Please note that this information is based on standard timetables and is intended as a general guide. Schedules are subject to change without notice, and delays may occur due to weather or other circumstances. We recommend checking the latest timetables in advance and allowing plenty of time in your schedule. The theater cannot be held responsible for any impact on your travel plans.

How do I get to the theater from Kyoto's main sightseeing areas?
The theater is directly connected to Kyoto Station's Hachijo Exit via underground passage. Approximate travel times from key areas:
・Fushimi Inari shrine: 5 minutes on the JR Nara Line
・Gion: 5 minutes by taxi, 20-30 minutes by bus
・Arashiyama: 20 minutes on the JR Sagano Line
・Kiyomizudera Temple: 30 minutes by bus (including walk to bus stop)
・Shijo Karasuma / Nishiki Market area: 5 minutes by Karasuma subway line
