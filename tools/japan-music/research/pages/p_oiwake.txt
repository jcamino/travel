東京・浅草 民謡酒場 和ノ家追分
浅草の老舗民謡酒場として知られた追分が、先代女将・服部光子の遺志を
受け継ぎ、令和の時代と共に新たにリニューアルオープン致しました。
津軽三味線全国チャンピオンを筆頭に、日々鍛錬を重ねる若手演奏家たちによる演奏、民謡、舞踊で浅草の夜を彩ります。
季節のお料理、お酒と共に是非一度「民謡酒場」を体感してみてください。

about
Building on our more than half-century history as the premier spot to enjoy traditional Japanese folk music in Tokyo, Kazunoya Oiwake has now re-opened, and on the very first day of the Reiwa Era. Among our performers include a number of Tsugaru shamisen national champions, and they are complemented by younger performers that strive to reach the reach the top of their arts while reaching your hearts, with traditional Japanese folksongs, dancing, and more. Add to that seasonal dining and a bit of sake, and we are sure you will enjoy your visit to our minyo sakaba (folksong drinkery). You will certainly want to tell your friends about us. 
But, better yet, bring them along with you when you visit.
