# Fourteen Nights · phone walk

Once per build that is shared, on the iPhone and an Android, five minutes. These are the promises in
the page's Housekeeping that no headless browser can vouch for. Tick them in the commit or the message
that shares the build.

## Install

- [ ] **C1** Safari: Share, Add to Home Screen. The icon is the lit window, the name is "Nights". Chrome on Android offers Install app.
- [ ] **C2** Opens standalone, dark status bar, and the bottom bar clears the home indicator (safe-area inset).
- [ ] **C3** Airplane mode, then open from the home screen: the whole page and every photograph are there. The weather line says "offline · forecast kept from".

## Touch

- [ ] **C4** Tap the bar, drag a finger across the fifteen windows: the label previews, release navigates, the card lands under the top edge.
- [ ] **C5** Double-tap on text does not zoom the page.
- [ ] **C6** In the lightbox, pinch zooms the photograph; a horizontal swipe changes it; tapping the black closes it.
- [ ] **C7** Open a map plate: the badge shows, the header reads "loading the full-resolution map…" then the pixel width; pinch in on the fine text.
- [ ] **C8** Rotate to landscape and back: nothing overflows, the bar stays put.

## Share

- [ ] **C9** Type a line in a night log, tap Postcard: the share sheet opens with a 1200×900 dark card, the numeral whole, the line under the picture. Send it to yourself on Signal.
- [ ] **C10** Paste the page's link into Signal: the preview shows the Erhai card and the title Fourteen Nights.

## Accessibility

- [ ] **C11** VoiceOver / TalkBack: the bar announces "Which night", the panel "Choose a day", a photo "Open map: …", the lightbox as a dialog.
- [ ] **C12** Text size at the largest accessibility step: day cards and the night-log inputs do not clip, the bar still shows two lines.
- [ ] **C13** Lamp set to noon in daylight: readable outdoors; the page is still dark.
- [ ] **C14** A new build pushed while the page is open: on the next open it is simply there, no flash of the old page, no reload while typing.
