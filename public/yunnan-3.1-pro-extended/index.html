Yunnan 2026

Icíar, Gabriela & Javier

Thursday 24 September to Thursday 8 October 2026.

A journey through the western borderlands of Yunnan: from the powerful ancient Buddhist kingdoms of Dali to the Naxi heartland of Lijiang, upward to the Tibetan plateau in Shangri-La, and down along the Southwestern Silk Road to Tengchong near the Myanmar border.

This route follows fragments of the Tea Horse Road (Chama Dao), a centuries-old trade network that transported Pu'er tea to Tibet and sturdy warhorses to China. You will traverse altitudes from 1,640m up to 3,160m, passing through the intersections of Bai, Naxi, and Tibetan cultures.

Day 1 Icíar's Birthday

Thursday, 24 September • Dali
Elevation: 1,980 m
  • Arrive 18:30 in Dali (direct flight from Beijing)
  • Sleep Spirited Silky Inn (Ancient Dali City)

Welcome to Yunnan. The transfer from Dali Airport to your hotel is self-arranged. Take a leisure walk around Dali Ancient Town this evening.

The Kingdom of Dali was a powerful, independent Buddhist state from 937 until 1253, famously holding off the Song Dynasty armies for centuries.

Day 2 Mid-Autumn Festival

Friday, 25 September • Dali Exploration
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep Spirited Silky Inn (Ancient Dali City)

Today marks the beginning of the three-day Mid-Autumn Festival holiday (Sept 25–27). Expect the ancient streets to be alive with domestic travelers.

Explore Fengyangyi Village, Xizhou Ancient Town, Panxi S-Bay, and Zhoucheng Tie-dye Village. Enjoy the scenery of Erhai Lake and the unique culture of the local Bai ethnic group.

Day 3 Javier Arrives

Saturday, 26 September • Dali to Lijiang / Shuhe
Elevation: 2,400 m
  • Transport Private transfer to Lijiang
  • Sleep Haitang Bay Resort Pillow Mountain Xigu Snow Mountain View Garden Beauty Villa (Shuhe)

Enjoy a free morning in Dali before your private transfer to Lijiang. You'll settle in Shuhe Ancient Town, a quieter counterpart to Lijiang's main hub.

Javier joins the journey tonight, landing at Lijiang airport at 20:50 (in from Osaka).

Day 4

Sunday, 27 September • Jade Dragon Snow Mountain
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep Haitang Bay Resort (Shuhe)

Head to the imposing Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. You'll visit Spruce Meadow and the brilliant turquoise waters of Blue Moon Valley, followed by the spectacular Impression Lijiang cultural show.

Lijiang was recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage site in 1997, a listing that transformed it from a sleepy regional center to a powerhouse of Chinese domestic tourism.

Day 5

Monday, 28 September • Yuhu & Baisha
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep Haitang Bay Resort (Shuhe)

Explore Yuhu Village and Baisha Ancient Town to experience authentic Naxi culture and countryside scenery. The Naxi people are famous for their Dongba script, the world's only living pictographic writing system.

Yuhu was the long-time home of Joseph Rock, the Austrian-American explorer and botanist who documented the region for National Geographic from 1922 to 1949, introducing western Yunnan to the world.

Day 6

Tuesday, 29 September • Shuhe Leisure
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep Haitang Bay Resort (Shuhe)

A day for slow-paced exploration around Shuhe Ancient Town. Enjoy cafés, local markets, and photography spots. Take this day for gentle altitude adaptation before heading higher.

Day 7

Wednesday, 30 September • Tiger Leaping Gorge to Shangri-La
Elevation: 3,160 m
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep Shangri-La Half View Vacation Homestay (Dukezong Ancient Town)
Altitude Warning (3,160m): Shangri-La sits on the Tibetan plateau. Walk slowly, stay hydrated, and avoid alcohol for the first 24 hours to prevent Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS).

Drive to Upper Tiger Leaping Gorge to experience scenic sections of the classic gorge trail—one of the deepest gorges in the world. Continue onward in the afternoon to check in at Dukezong Ancient Town.

Day 8 National Day

Thursday, 1 October • Shangri-La
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep Shangri-La Half View Vacation Homestay

Today is China's National Day, marking the start of the week-long "Golden Week". The scale of domestic travel is staggering, with hundreds of millions on the move.

Visit Pudacuo National Park, Songtsam Monastery, and (weather permitting) Napa Lake. Diqing is a Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, offering a window into Tibetan plateau scenery and culture.

In 2001, to boost tourism and capitalize on James Hilton's 1933 novel 'Lost Horizon', the Chinese government officially renamed this county from Zhongdian to Shangri-La.

Day 9 Friday Market

Friday, 2 October • Shangri-La to Shaxi
Elevation: 2,100 m
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep Daoxiang residents' accommodation (or possibly He Garden Hotel)

A scenic drive southward through western Yunnan's mountain landscapes brings you to Shaxi Ancient Town.

The Friday Market: From about 10:00 into the early afternoon, the surrounding villagers gather to trade. Shaxi's Sideng Square is recognized as the last surviving ancient market square on the Tea Horse Road, retaining the exact layout from its caravan days.

Day 10

Saturday, 3 October • Shaxi
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep Daoxiang residents' accommodation

A day of in-depth exploration in Shaxi. Visit Sideng Street, the Ancient Theater Stage, and Yujin Bridge. Take your time exploring temples, old streets, and countryside villages on this historic Tea Horse route.

Day 11

Sunday, 4 October • Shaxi to Baoshan
Elevation: 1,650 m
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep Vienna International Hotel (Baoshan)

Embark on a scenic long-distance drive southwestward. Baoshan serves as a strategic overnight stop to avoid an overly taxing transfer.

Day 12

Monday, 5 October • Baoshan to Tengchong
Elevation: 1,640 m
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep A panoramic boutique Inn (Tengchong)

Continue the drive to Tengchong, entering a region defined by its volcanic mountain scenery and peaceful countryside. Tengchong sits near the Myanmar border and was a vital node on the Southwestern Silk Road.

Day 13 Javier Departs

Tuesday, 6 October • Tengchong
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep A panoramic boutique Inn

Visit Heshun Ancient Town, famed for its elegant architecture built by wealthy overseas Chinese and jade merchants.

Farewell Javier: Leaving around midday, Javier flies out of Tengchong airport (located on a hilltop outside town) at 15:20 on Sichuan Airlines to Chengdu, and onward to Los Angeles.

History note: Tengchong was a major strategic theater in WWII. The 1944 Battle of Tengchong saw Chinese forces, supplied by the perilous "Hump" airlift over the Himalayas, fight a bloody urban campaign to retake the walled city from Japanese occupation.

Day 14

Wednesday, 7 October • Tengchong
  • Transport Private car & driver
  • Sleep A panoramic boutique Inn

Your final full day in Yunnan. Enjoy free leisure activities. Optional visits include the volcanic park, wetland park, or experiencing Tengchong's renowned hot springs and spas.

Day 15

Thursday, 8 October • Tengchong to Dali to Shanghai
  • Transport Private transfer (4–5 hours) to Dali Airport
  • Depart 17:40 flight from Dali to Shanghai

A long but scenic drive retraces your path east to Dali Airport in time for your 17:40 flight to Shanghai. End of services.

Historical & Geopolitical Sources

  • Dali Kingdom & Mongol Conquest: The independent Kingdom of Dali ruled until Kublai Khan's conquest in 1253, formally bringing Yunnan under imperial Chinese administration. (Mote, F.W., Imperial China 900-1800, Harvard University Press, 1999).
  • Lijiang UNESCO Status: The Old Town of Lijiang was inscribed on the World Heritage List in 1997. (UNESCO World Heritage Centre, whc.unesco.org).
  • Shangri-La Renaming: The State Council of the PRC approved the renaming of Zhongdian County to Shangri-La in 2001 to bolster tourism. (China Daily / State Council archives, 2001).
  • Shaxi & The Tea Horse Road: Sideng Square in Shaxi is recognized by the World Monuments Fund as the last surviving ancient market square on the Tea Horse Road. (World Monuments Fund, wmf.org).
  • Battle of Tengchong (1944): Part of the Burma Road campaign where Chinese Expeditionary Forces, supported by US airlifts (The Hump), recaptured the city. (Mitter, Rana. Forgotten Ally: China's World War II, 2013).
