What to book, and when

Most of this trip needs no booking. The hotels, the car and five sets of tickets are already booked, and nearly everything else is sold at a window or on Trip.com the same day. A short list is different.

Every fact here was checked against a page on 19 September 2026 and carries its source. Where a page could not be read, the line says unverified. The days and the history are on the main page.

The message to the agency, ready to copy 
Hello,

A few things before we arrive, all in one message.

1. Could you add the Glacier Park cableway (4,506 m) for the three of us on our free day, Tuesday 29 September, with the earliest slot you can get? We would keep Spruce Meadow and the show on the 27th as planned. Would we need a second park entrance ticket, and does the rule of one purchase every ninety days get in the way? We understand the agency quota closes about three days before.

2. Which Impression Lijiang showtime and which cableway slot do you hold for us?

3. Could you confirm the names and passport numbers registered on each real-name ticket, and whether the 50 yuan Lijiang old town fee is included?

4. On 2 October, could we leave Shangri-La early enough to reach Shaxi by about 12:30? It is the Friday market and it winds down in mid-afternoon.

5. In Tengchong, could the driver book the tethered balloon at the volcano park for the morning of 7 October, and make the You Yunnan reservation for the war memorial museum with our passports?

6. We read that the G56 expressway between Shunbi and Pingpo was cut by a rockfall on 27 August and reopened on one carriageway. Could you check it before 4 October and again before 8 October, and set the pickup on 8 October for 08:00?

Many thanks,
Icíar and Gabriela

Music

Night by night. There is no concert ticket to buy anywhere on this route.

Showstart, where China's clubs sell their gigs, lists 41 venues in Dali and 19 in Lijiang and not one event in any of your towns between 24 September and 7 October; searches for Shangri-La, Shaxi, Jianchuan, Baoshan, Tengchong and Heshun came back empty as well. Damai, the other big seller, blocks every kind of automated reading and was not checked. No 2026 edition of the Lijiang Snow Mountain or COART festivals was found, and the one festival Lijiang has announced, Lychee Cloud, is on 17 and 18 October. So the music on this trip is the old kind: ensembles of elderly men playing scripture music, circle dances in the squares, and bars with a singer. Two evenings stand out, the Naxi concert in Lijiang and Heshun on 5 October.

What you are hearing

  • Dongjing 洞经. Daoist ritual music kept by lay associations that honoured Wenchang, the god of letters, and named for the scripture they chanted. Pipa, sanxian, reed pipe and zithers all play the same tune at once, each in its own way. The Naxi of Lijiang and the Bai of Dali and Shaxi have it from the same source. Wikipedia
  • Baisha Xiyue 白沙细乐. A Naxi suite of instruments, song and dance of which eight of ten tunes survive. One story makes it Kublai's parting gift to the Naxi chief; another calls it mourning music, and it was played at funerals. Flutes, reed pipe, the plucked sugudu and small pipa, eight players or more. Yunnan heritage register
  • Dabenqu 大本曲. Bai sung storytelling: one singer and one sanxian carrying a whole long tale in the Bai language. Its best known bearer, Zhao Piding, is from Zuoyi village in Xizhou. Xuexi
  • Guozhuang and xianzi 锅庄 弦子. Guozhuang is the Tibetan ring dance sung by its own dancers. In xianzi the men lead while bowing a horsehair fiddle and the women dance with long sleeves, slow at first and ending in spins; it belongs to Deqin and Weixi, north of where you sleep. Shangri-La news, Jul 2024

Paying with a passport

Which channel works for whom.

  • Trip.com, in English. Takes a foreign phone number, a passport and a foreign card. Type the name exactly as it is printed in the machine-readable lines at the foot of the passport page, and carry the passport itself to the gate. On this trip it sells Pudacuo, Songzanlin, the Three Pagodas, Cangshan, the Lijiang shows, Angsana, the Lotus spring, Beihai and the volcano park. Trip.com's own guide, Jul 2026
  • Alipay and WeChat Pay. Both register with a passport and a foreign card. Payments of 200 yuan or more with a foreign card carry a 3 per cent fee; below 200 it is waived. Didi runs inside Alipay in English. Beijing city guide, May 2025 · Didi for visitors, May 2026
  • Mini-programs. The official ones for Jade Dragon, Heshun and the war museum are built for a Chinese identity card and a Chinese phone number, and often fail a foreigner at the verification step. Do not count on them. a 2026 guide
  • The window. Heshun, Shibaoshan, Xingjiao Temple, Rehai and the gorge all have one. Passports in hand.
  • The driver and the agency. For anything capped and real-name, which means Jade Dragon above all, and for anything sold by telephone: the balloon, Bailian, the museum reservation, a restaurant table. This is the normal route, not a favour.
