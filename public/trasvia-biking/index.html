Road Rides from Trasvía

Nine rides from the front door for a strong rider — coastal rollers over the rías, near-empty cols in the Nansa and Saja valleys, and the long road through the La Hermida gorge into the Picos de Europa. Friday 14 – Sunday 16 August 2026.

The queen stage, drawn to scale: Trasvía → Fuente Dé, ≈80 km one-way. Flat to Panes, false-flat through the gorge to Potes, then 22.7 km up the Liébana — the last 3 km after Espinama ramp toward 9%.

Weather, holiday & the smart order

The three days

Saturday 15 is the Assumption — a national holiday that shuts shops and fills the coast road and the Picos funnel. The forecast (AEMET, taken 14 Aug 09:05) shapes the rest.

Fri 14 Aug

21–25 °C · rain 50% from evening
wind SW→NW 10–15 km/h
sun 07:20–21:24

Dry until evening. Coast day — the East Rollers or the Three Rías — and be back before the evening rain. Save the mountains for the weekend.

Sat 15 Aug Holiday

20–25 °C · rain 95% pre-dawn, improving midday
wind NW 15 km/h, easing
sun 07:21–21:22

Wet roads early, jammed coast all day. Go inland once it dries: the Carmona loop or Palombera — the CA-180/182/280 network is the quiet corner of Cantabria today.

Sun 16 Aug

20–26 °C · light rain possible early, drier PM
wind N 10 km/h
sun 07:22–21:21

The big one. If dawn is dry, roll by 08:00 for Fuente Dé or the Ozalba + Hoz loop and beat the gorge traffic. If it isn't, Palombera from midday.

Forecast is a snapshot — recheck at AEMET Comillas (id 39024) each morning, and the gorge road status at obrasn621.es before any Picos ride.

150–170 km · the Picos & Liébana

Big days

Every big day funnels through the Desfiladero de La Hermida — 20 km of limestone gorge on the N-621, rebuilt and widened for cyclists in works finished summer 2025, with no closures in force on 14 Aug. Lights for the short tunnels, early starts to beat the tourist wave.

Queen stage · out-and-back

Fuente Dé

The Vuelta's road into the Picos: the full gorge to Potes, then 22.7 km up the Liébana to the cable-car wall where Contador sealed the 2012 Vuelta.

Key climbPotes → Fuente Dé: 22.7 km · 809 m+ · 3.6% avg · 9.1% max — easy to Espinama, then the ramp (climbfinder)
Totals≈170 km · ≈2,300–2,600 m+ Estimate
FuelSan Vicente, Panes, Potes (towns). Cafetería at the Fuente Dé cable-car base Confirmed (t. 942 736 610). Espinama / La Hermida bars Unconfirmed
Best daySun 16 with an 08:00 start. Avoid Sat 15 — holiday peak in the Picos funnel.
  • Lights required: short tunnels in the gorge — front white + rear red is the law in tunnels.
  • Gorge traffic triples in summer (baseline ~2,500 vehicles/day) — start ≤08:00 and be back through the narrows before the midday wave. Fuente Dé is the busiest climb here (rider votes: "ok", not "great"); mornings are calmer.
  • Good news The 17.2 km widening of the gorge road was finished summer 2025, explicitly built to make the road usable by cyclists — pull-outs and wider carriageway throughout.

Roads & events status

What's open, what's jammed

  • Open La Hermida gorge (N-621): no traffic cuts in force 14 Aug (official works site live feed). A March 2026 rockfall at the Panes entrance was cleared; rockfall risk is inherent — recheck obrasn621.es the morning of any gorge ride.
  • Unconfirmed CA-131, CA-135, CA-180, CA-182, CA-280, N-634: no roadworks found in searches, but the regional live-status site was unreachable. DGT's live map needs a browser: infocar.dgt.es/etraffic.
  • Avoid Torrelavega centre, 13–23 Aug: Virgen Grande fiestas — street closures and saturated approaches, worst Sat 15 (music stages 13:00–24:00).
  • La Vuelta 2026 starts 22 Aug in Monaco — nowhere near Cantabria this weekend, and no local race or cicloturista was found on these roads for 14–16 Aug.
  • Maybe San Vicente may hold Asunción/San Roque fiestas 14–16 Aug (sources conflict) — expect crowds either way; tourist office +34 942 71 00 12.

DGT — in force this window

Rules of the road

  • Helmet: compulsory on interurban roads (exceptions: prolonged climbs, extreme heat, medical grounds).
  • Two abreast is legal on the open road — single out on blind curves and when traffic stacks up.
  • Being passed: drivers owe you ≥1.5 m, a full lane change on multi-lane roads, and a 20 km/h speed reduction — ride assuming they don't know this.
  • Shoulder: use the arcén where it exists and is rideable; otherwise take what carriageway you need.
  • Tunnels & night: front white + rear red lights, and a reflective garment visible at 150 m on interurban roads — the gorge tunnels count as tunnels.
  • Emergencies: 112.
