Monument · 3.0 km · 5 min

El Capricho de Gaudí

Gaudí's 1883–85 orientalist summer villa — minaret tower, sunflower tiles, Persian and Mudéjar flourishes. One of only three Gaudí buildings outside Catalonia, and 2026 is officially "Gaudí Year".

Hours (Aug)Daily 10:00–21:00, last entry 20:30 Confirmed
English toursDaily 12:30 and 16:30 Confirmed · advance booking recommended (official notice)
Spanish toursRoughly hourly 11:00–18:00 Unconfirmed — confirm at +34 942 720 365
Price7 EUR with audioguide (5 reduced, 3 child 7–12, under 7 free) · 10 EUR guided tour (8 reduced, 6 child) Confirmed
Bookingtickets.elcaprichodegaudi.com
From Trasvía3.0 km · ~5 min drive, or ~30 min on foot
  • Allow ~1 h: the visit itself is ~30 min with audioguide (~45 min guided) plus the gardens.
  • Quietest windows: right at 10:00, or 18:30–20:00 when the coach crowds are gone and the evening light hits the sunflower tiles.
  • The English tour slots are the scarce item in August — book before you arrive.
  • Evening slots are the ones to take: after ~18:30 the coach crowds are gone, and the last entry is 20:30.
  • Minor caveat: some third-party sites list a 10:30 opening; the official municipal August schedule says 10:00.

The monumental hilltop seminary (1883, Martorell) whose facade and doorway are by Lluís Domènech i Montaner — one of the great Catalan-Modernisme ensembles outside Catalonia, with sweeping views over Comillas and the coast.

Mountains · 82.3 km · 1 h 20

Teleférico de Fuente Dé (Picos de Europa)

A 3 min 40 s cable-car leap up ~750 m of vertical rock to El Cable at 1,823 m — instant high-mountain scenery, with walks into the Áliva meadows from the top. The drive there through the La Hermida gorge is a highlight in itself.

Hours (Aug)Daily 08:00–19:00 · last ascent 18:45 · last descent 18:50 Confirmed
PriceReturn 30 EUR adult / 15 EUR child 4–12 (one-way 17 / 8, station-only) Confirmed — ignore the widespread outdated 17–20 EUR figures online
Bookingentradas.telefericofuentede.com — dated tickets with 30-min entry slots, online sales close 24 h ahead; same-day only at the base ticket office
From Trasvía82.3 km · ~1 h 20 (A-8 to Unquera, N-621 through the gorge, Potes, CA-185)

Low tide · free · best Mon–Tue

Rasa mareal — the low-tide coastal walk

At low tide the sea peels back off a flat rocky platform of folded, layered strata between Comillas and Oyambre: tide pools full of crabs, urchins, anemones and the odd octopus. Be honest about the timing this week — Monday and Tuesday are the only strong days, and by Thursday the sea barely uncovers it.

RouteDrop onto the platform at Playa de Comillas and head west toward the Ría de La Rabia and Oyambre; ~4–5 km one way. Clifftop path (Senda Costera, ~5.5 km circular) runs parallel as an escape/return route.

  • Saturday's low at 19:30 sits in the last light before a 21:10 sunset — the least useful tide for rock-pooling, the best one for photographs.
  • Sunday flips to a morning low: the only pre-breakfast window of the week, and it pairs with the drive to Altamira for the free Sunday entry.
  • At the Oyambre end look for the retreating dune front (~1.3 m/yr), occasionally exposed ~5,000-year-old tree stumps, and the "Pájaro Amarillo" monument to a 1929 transatlantic flight's emergency landing.
  • Heights are relative to mean sea level (that's why lows are negative). Sunrise 07:24 → 07:31, sunset 21:17 → 21:08 across the week.

Tides verified 16 Aug 2026 on tablademareas.com (Comillas), cross-checked with tides4fishing.com (San Vicente) — every tide time across the seven days agrees within 1 min and 0.1 m.

Monday to Sunday, tide-aware

Suggested plan

Four fixed points shape the week: Monday closes both Sobrellano and Altamira, so day one is the Seminario, the coast and the town; the rasa mareal is strongest on Mon–Tue and fades to nothing by Friday; and the free Altamira windows land on the final weekend. As before, Sobrellano and the Seminario sell no same-day online tickets, and the 11:30 walking tours clash with El Capricho's 12:30 English slot — so take the 16:30.

One of you is working days. Items marked ☾ start before 09:00 or after 18:00, so they work around a normal working day — every day has at least one. Use the toggle to strip the plan down to just those.

Monday 17 — the closed day, so take the coast

  • Morning Book the week online now: Seminario and Sobrellano (never same-day), La Portilla, the Capricho English slot, and a Fuente Dé cabin if you want the Picos — its online sales shut 24 h ahead and August slots go days early.
  • 10:15 Seminario Mayor guided tour — the one big monument open today, and the grounds and view come free.
  • 12:35–16:05 Rasa mareal at its best (low 14:19, coefficient 69). This is the strongest tide of your whole stay — don't save it for later in the week, it only gets weaker.
  • 16:30 El Capricho — English guided tour.
  • 17:30 Elia's free walking tour (Moncho's 18:00 as alternative).
  • ☾ 18:30–20:30 El Capricho with the audioguide (7 EUR, last entry 20:30) — the version of the visit that fits a working day, and the best light on the sunflower tiles.
  • ☾ 19:00–21:00 Last night of "Los colores de Julio de Pablo" at the old Town Hall — free, and gone tomorrow.
  • Note Sobrellano and the Museo de Altamira are both closed today. Don't drive to Santillana.

Tuesday 18 — Sobrellano opens

  • 10:1x Sobrellano guided tour — first chance of the week. Pair with the Seminario at ~12:4x if you skipped it yesterday (90–120 min spacing rule).
  • 13:15–16:45 Second-best tide of the week (low 15:00) — platform walk or Oyambre.
  • ☾ Afternoon Alternative: La Portilla, the indiano mansion — 16:30 or 18:05 slot, 70 min, 12 EUR, max 15 people, booking obligatory.
  • ☾ Evening Book presentation, "Tras el rastro de Madrid" — time and venue unpublished, ask the tourist office.

Wednesday 19 — walk day

  • Morning Monte Corona (8 km, 2 h 30, moderate) from Rioturbio — coastal oak and low-altitude beech, down to the Ría de La Rabia. Or Ruiseñada (8 km, 2 h 30, easy) if you want it gentler.
  • 14:00–17:30 Low tide 15:46, coefficient 43 — thinner platform now; treat it as a beach afternoon with rock pools rather than the main event.
  • ☾ 19:30 Elia's evening walking tour — one of only three 19:30 departures this week.
  • All day Swap: El Soplao (~30 min drive) — book the tourist visit online a day ahead; 12–14 °C inside, bring a fleece.

Thursday 20 — the Picos

  • All day Teleférico de Fuente Dé (~1 h 20 drive) — book the timed slot by Wednesday, aim for the 08:00–09:00 cabins to beat the queue and the afternoon cloud, lunch in Potes on the way back.
  • 15:00–18:30 If you stay local: low 16:44 but only −0.8 m — marginal for the rasa. Playa de Comillas or Oyambre is the better call.
  • ☾ Evening El Capricho stays open to 21:00 (last entry 20:30) — the quietest hour to see it, and the sunflower tiles catch the low light.
  • Note Elia's schedule advertises a 19:30 today but the booking engine returns no seats — assume 11:30 and 17:30 only.

Friday 21 — market morning

  • 09:00–14:00 Friday market at the Campa de Sobrellano — 30+ stalls of local food, textiles and flowers, below the palace.
  • Midday Ruta Costera on foot (7 km, ~2 h 22, easy) out past the Mirador de Santa Lucía — the tide is the weakest of the week (coefficient 29), so stay on the clifftop path rather than the rocks.
  • Afternoon Good day for Cabárceno (~1 h) — a full 4–6 h; birds-of-prey demos 12:00, 15:45, 17:30.
  • ☾ 19:30 Elia's evening tour, or Moncho's 18:00.

Saturday 22 — Altamira free from 14:00

  • Morning Book Sobrellano/Seminario for today if you haven't — Saturday is the busiest day of your week at both.
  • From 14:00 Museo de Altamira free for everyone until 20:00 — but free tickets are desk-only and first-come, so join the queue early afternoon; then wander Santillana del Mar.
  • ☾ 17:45–21:00 Low tide 19:30 into a 21:10 sunset — the golden-hour coast walk. Little rock exposed, excellent light.
  • ☾ 19:30 Or Elia's evening walking tour back in town.

Sunday 23 — early tide, free museum

  • ☾ 07:31–09:40 The week's only morning low (07:54) — a quiet pre-breakfast platform walk with the beach to yourselves.
  • 09:30 Drive to Altamira: free all day, open 09:30–18:00 (an August extension on the usual 15:00 Sunday close). Desk-only tickets, so arrive at opening.
  • Afternoon Lunch in Santillana, or back for La Portilla's 16:30 slot.
  • 17:30 Elia's walking tour — last chance; all three guides run today, unlike last week.
  • Note Sobrellano's Sunday hours are shorter (last tour 17:10), and it closes again on Monday 24 if you're staying on.
