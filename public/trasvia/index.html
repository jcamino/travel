In Comillas

The three big monuments (El Capricho, Palacio de Sobrellano, Seminario Mayor) sit within a few hundred meters of each other, 2–3 km from Trasvía. Important: Sobrellano and the Seminario do not sell same-day tickets online — book the day before at centros.culturadecantabria.com, or take your chances at the box office.

El Capricho de Gaudí

Gaudí's 1883–85 orientalist summer villa — minaret tower, sunflower tiles, Persian and Mudéjar flourishes. One of only three Gaudí buildings outside Catalonia, and 2026 is officially "Gaudí Year".

Hours (Aug)Daily 10:00–21:00, last entry 20:30 Confirmed
English toursDaily 12:30 and 16:30 Confirmed · advance booking recommended (official notice)
Spanish toursRoughly hourly 11:00–18:00 Unconfirmed — confirm at +34 942 720 365
Price7 EUR with audioguide (5 reduced, 3 child 7–12, under 7 free) · 10 EUR guided tour (8 reduced, 6 child) Confirmed
Bookingtickets.elcaprichodegaudi.com
Distance3.0 km · ~5 min drive (or ~30 min walk)
  • Allow ~1 h: the visit itself is ~30 min with audioguide (~45 min guided) plus the gardens.
  • Quietest windows: right at 10:00, or 18:30–20:00 when the coach crowds are gone and the evening light hits the sunflower tiles.
  • The English tour slots are the scarce item in August — book before you arrive.
  • Minor caveat: some third-party sites list a 10:30 opening; the official municipal August schedule says 10:00.

Seminario Mayor — former Universidad Pontificia

The monumental hilltop seminary (1883, Martorell) whose facade and doorway are by Lluís Domènech i Montaner — one of the great Catalan-Modernisme ensembles outside Catalonia, with sweeping views over Comillas and the coast. The closest monument to Trasvía.

  • Even without a tour, the grounds (09:30–19:00) and the view over town are worth the stop.

Cueva El Soplao

One of Spain's most spectacular show caves — an extraordinary density of "eccentric" helictite formations reached through old mining galleries, with coastal views from the 540 m entrance.

Santillana del Mar + Museo de Altamira

One of Spain's best-preserved medieval towns — cobbles, Romanesque Colegiata, stone manors — with the museum of the "Sistine Chapel of cave art" 2 km up the hill: the Neocueva is an exact replica of Altamira's painted ceiling.

Museum hoursThu 13 & Fri 14: 09:30–20:00 · Sat 15 (holiday): 09:30–20:00, explicitly open Confirmed · Sun 16: 09:30–18:00 · ticket desk closes 30 min before · closed Mondays
Price3 EUR (1.50 reduced) · free for everyone Sat from 14:00 and all day Sun — free tickets are desk-only, first-come Confirmed
BookingPaid advance tickets (timed Neocueva slot included) via the official Santander ticketing platform — must be bought at least one day ahead, never same-day; arrive 15 min early or lose the slot
Distance20.1 km · ~25 min drive (town and museum are 2 km apart)
The real cave: the old Friday lottery no longer exists — original-cave access is limited to a long-closed waiting list, so the Neocueva replica is the visit (and it's excellent).
  • The museum itself recommends booking ahead online in summer; August slots sell out. For the free Sat-afternoon/Sunday entry, queue at the desk early (tickets from 09:30).
  • Neocueva entry reportedly runs in groups of ~20 every ~25 min Third-party.
  • Santillana town is free to wander but pedestrianized — park at the edge (paid lot at the entrance; free options at the bus station ~500 m and polideportivo ~1.5 km).

Teleférico de Fuente Dé (Picos de Europa)

A 3 min 40 s cable-car leap up ~750 m of vertical rock to El Cable at 1,823 m — instant high-mountain scenery, with walks into the Áliva meadows from the top. The drive there through the La Hermida gorge is a highlight in itself.

Hours (Aug)Daily 08:00–19:00 · last ascent 18:45 · last descent 18:50 Confirmed
PriceReturn 30 EUR adult / 15 EUR child 4–12 (one-way 17 / 8, station-only) Confirmed — ignore the widespread outdated 17–20 EUR figures online
EnglishNo tour involved — it's a ride; signage bilingual enough
Bookingentradas.telefericofuentede.com — dated tickets with 30-min entry slots, online sales close 24 h ahead; same-day only at the base ticket office
Distance82.3 km · ~1 h 20 (A-8 to Unquera, N-621 through the gorge, Potes, CA-185)
August queue reality: walk-up waits of 2–3 h are routinely reported, and online slots sell out days ahead — book now for the earliest slot you can get (it opens at 08:00; mornings also beat the afternoon cloud). The descent has no timed slot — it's a first-come queue, so start heading down well before the 18:50 last cabin.
  • From the top: stroll the El Cable viewpoint, or walk down through the Puertos de Áliva to Espinama and back to the car park (~13–14 km, mostly downhill track).
  • It's typically 10 °C cooler up top — bring a layer. Wind closes the cable car with no notice: call +34 942 736 610 or check the site that morning.
  • Stop-offs: Santa María de Lebeña (9th-c. Mozarabic church, 1 km off the N-621) on the way in; Potes (Torre del Infantado, orujo shops) for lunch on the way back.
  • Possible N-621 roadworks with traffic cuts this summer Unconfirmed — check infocar.dgt.es and pad your slot time.

Parque de la Naturaleza de Cabárceno

A drive-through wildlife park in a vast former open-cast iron mine: ~20 km of roads winding between semi-free-roaming elephants (one of Europe's leading herds), a basin of 60+ brown bears, gorillas, rhinos and giraffes among red-rock karst.

Hours (Aug)09:00–18:00 (ticket-window close; you may stay until sunset) · in-park cable car 09:30–18:30, last ascent 18:00 Confirmed

Beaches, surf & the low-tide walk

Trasvía sits on the coast road between Comillas and Oyambre — both beaches are minutes away. This week has spring tides (coefficients 82–101), the best possible conditions for the low-tide coastal walk.

Playa de Comillas

The town beach: ~900 m of fine golden sand with a promenade to the harbor, gentle beginner-friendly waves.

Distance~2 km · ~5 min by car from Trasvía (or walk down through town)
LifeguardsCruz Roja daily 11:30–19:30 (15 Jun–15 Sep) Confirmed
FacilitiesBlue Flag · toilets, showers, sunshade/lounger rental · fully adapted access (amphibious chair available) · no dogs
PriceFree (paid parking lot adjacent)
  • El Capricho de Gaudí is only ~700 m away — easy to combine.
  • The parking lot fills very fast in August; arrive before ~09:30 or walk from town.
  • Sheltered, gentle surf — experienced surfers head to Oyambre instead.

Playa de Oyambre (& Playa de Gerra)

Nearly 2 km of wild sand backed by live dunes and the La Rabia estuary marshes, inside the Parque Natural de Oyambre — a designated World Surfing Reserve, with the Picos de Europa on the horizon.

Distance~3–4 km · ~5–7 min by car from Trasvía Estimate
LifeguardsJuly–August only
FacilitiesSeasonal beach bar + portable toilets at the main access · adapted access · otherwise unspoiled
PriceFree · parking lots off the CA-236 (last stretch partly unpaved); full by mid-morning in August
  • Birdwatching in the La Rabia estuary: herons, spoonbills, cormorants.
  • Playa de Gerra is the quieter western continuation of the same sand (access at La Gerruca, km 2.5 of the CA-236).
  • Never walk on the dunes — the system is protected and actively eroding.

Rasa mareal — the low-tide coastal walk

At low tide the sea peels back off a flat rocky platform of folded, layered strata between Comillas and Oyambre: tide pools full of crabs, urchins, anemones and the odd octopus. This week's spring tides expose it exceptionally well.

RouteDrop onto the platform at Playa de Comillas and head west toward the Ría de La Rabia and Oyambre; ~4–5 km one way. Clifftop path (Senda Costera, ~5.5 km circular) runs parallel as an escape/return route.

Best windowRoughly low tide ±1.75 h — see table below

Safety: go out on the falling tide and be off the rocks within ~1.5–2 h after low — with coefficients this high the flood comes back fast and can cut you off against the cliffs. Closed non-slip shoes; skip it in swell or rain. If the tide catches you, climb to the Senda Costera clifftop path.
  • At the Oyambre end look for the retreating dune front (~1.3 m/yr), occasionally exposed ~5,000-year-old tree stumps, and the "Pájaro Amarillo" monument to a 1929 transatlantic flight's emergency landing.
  • Heights are relative to mean sea level (that's why lows are negative). Sunrise ~07:20, sunset ~21:20 all four days.

Suggested plan, Thursday–Sunday

Built around three fixed points: the 11:30 walking tours end ~13:00 and clash with the 12:30 English tour at El Capricho (so English Capricho slots are always the 16:30); Sobrellano and the Seminario sell no same-day online tickets; and the low-tide walk window slides ~40 min later each day. Sobrellano is closed Mondays — this week that only matters if you stay past Sunday.

Thursday 13 — settle in, tides & Gaudí

  • 09:45–13:15 Rasa mareal low-tide walk (low 11:35) or a first morning at Playa de Oyambre.
  • Midday Book ahead online for the rest of the stay: Sobrellano (Friday is nearly sold out), Seminario, Altamira, Capricho — and a Fuente Dé slot if you want the Picos (online sales close 24 h ahead and August slots sell out days early).
  • 16:30 El Capricho — English guided tour.
  • 17:30 / 19:30 Elia's free walking tour (Moncho's 18:00 as alternative).

Friday 14 — monuments day (or El Soplao)

  • 10:15 Seminario Mayor guided tour, then the grounds and the view.
  • 12:18 Low tide — short platform wander below the town, or beach time.
  • 16:10 Sobrellano guided tour (satisfies the 90–120 min spacing rule; book the moment you can — Friday had 1-seat sessions on 13 Aug).
  • 17:30 Alternative: Emilio's 2 h storytelling tour — his only departure this week.
  • All day Swap: El Soplao (~30 min drive) — book the tourist visit online a day ahead; fleece required.

Saturday 15 — holiday crowds, go early or local

  • 10:10 Sobrellano tour + 12:45 Seminario tour — the pairing that satisfies the spacing rule; or take the 11:30 free walking tour instead.
  • 11:00–14:45 Low-tide window (low 12:59) if you skipped the tours.
  • 16:30 El Capricho English tour (the 12:30 clashes with everything above).
  • Evening Oyambre for sunset; Elia's 19:30 walk as a nightcap.

Sunday 16 — Altamira free day & last tide

  • 09:15 Drive to Altamira — free all Sunday, desk-only tickets from 09:30, so arrive at opening; then wander Santillana del Mar.
  • 11:45–15:20 Back for the last (and latest) low-tide window (low 13:39), or lunch in Santillana.
  • 17:30 Elia's walking tour — the only guide running Sunday.
  • All day Big-mountain swap: Teleférico de Fuente Dé (~1 h 20 drive) — needs a timed slot booked by Friday; leave early for the 08:00–09:00 cabins and lunch in Potes on the way back.
  • Note Sobrellano's Sunday hours are shorter (last tour 17:10); Monday it's closed — Capricho (daily) and the Seminario (Mon–Sat) are the Monday options if you stay on.
