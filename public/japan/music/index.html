Fri 25 · 17:30 then 19:00 · Shimogamo 名月管絃祭 Meigetsu Kangen-sai, then Yasaka 祇園社観月祭 Gionsha Kangetsu-sai — two harvest-moon rites in one evening: shakuhachi, gagaku and koto from Shimogamo's 橋殿 by firelight, then bugaku on Yasaka's open-sided 舞殿 under the moon

Shimogamo Jinja (Demachiyanagi) 17:30–21:00, then Yasaka Jinja (Gion, 15 min walk from Kawaramachi) 19:00–21:00 · both free, no ticket, standing · Tier 1 · VERIFIED Shimogamo 2026 page, shrine notice, 21 Aug 2026

What happens. 25 Sept is 中秋の名月, the fifteenth night of the eighth lunar month (NAOJ). At Shimogamo, after the 17:30 rite, kagaribi are lit, susuki is set out and shakuhachi, a gagaku ensemble and koto play from the 橋殿, the bridge-hall over the stream, until 21:00. At Yasaka, at 19:00 the priests hold the rite on the 舞殿, the lantern-hung dance stage in the middle of the precinct; at 19:15 the year's winning poems on the set theme 松虫 (pine cricket) are chanted in the old 披講 manner; at 19:30 弥栄雅楽会, the shrine's own gagaku society, plays 管弦 and dances 舞楽 in full court costume under the moon; 20:00 生田流 koto and sangen; 20:30 the Gion lion dance; 20:45 the shrine's taiko group closes with 剣の舞. It is over by about 21:00, which suits your early airport train.

Why it is the pick. This is gagaku as an offering inside a Shinto rite, at the shrine that runs the Gion Festival, on the one night of the year the rite exists for. New York gets gagaku occasionally, in a concert hall. The precinct is open: no seat to buy, no line, a crowd of Kyoto families with dango.

The sequence. Leave Arashiyama by about 16:15, be at Shimogamo Jinja for the 名月管絃祭 at 17:30 (the rite, then the first shakuhachi and gagaku offerings from the 橋殿 by firelight, free, shrine page), walk to Demachiyanagi at about 18:35, Keihan to Gion-Shijō (5 min), and stand on the west side of Yasaka's 舞殿 by 19:10 for the 19:30 舞楽. Shimogamo runs to 21:00, so you trade its koto for Yasaka's bugaku, which is the better trade; if you only do one, Yasaka. If you would rather not cross town, Daikaku-ji's 観月の夕べ opens 17:30 where you already are (¥700; the ¥7,000 boat lottery closed in July) and Yasaka by 19:30 is about 50 minutes door to door. If it rains, both the 舞殿 and the 橋殿 are roofed.
